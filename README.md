## About the DOIs

The DOIs above were minted from releases of *this* repository, and all of them still resolve. The tags, releases and files they point to are kept here unchanged under `python-notebooks/`.

The concept DOI [10.5281/zenodo.14640807](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.14640807) is Zenodo's record for this whole bundled repository. It always resolves to the newest release, whichever notebook that happens to be. To cite a specific notebook, use its version DOI from the table.
